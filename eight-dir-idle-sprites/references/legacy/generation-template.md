# Generation template

아래 고정 스타일을 사용자의 설명 및 선택적인 연출 요청과 결합한다. 실제 참조 이미지를 함께 전달한다. 예시 등장인물의 고유 디자인은 설명에 있을 때만 사용한다.

## Prompt

Create the character or monster described below as a new member of the SAME GAME ART SET as the supplied bundled reference images. Match the reference king and character trio's visual language closely. Use the images as the art-style authority; use the user's description as the design authority.

USER DESIGN:
{user_description}

COMPOSITION AND DELIVERY:
{user_requested_view_pose_action_expression_count_layout_background_aspect_ratio_or_sensible_defaults}

FIXED ART STYLE:
- Use very round, broad, plump shapes, short squat proportions, a tiny compact torso and small rounded hands and feet. For humanoids, the head plus headgear occupies roughly 60–65% of the neutral standing silhouette; adapt naturally to perspective and movement. Keep the compact character construction recognizable across views.
- Preserve each monster's species and anatomy. Apply the same round forms and simplification to animals, blobs, flying creatures and multi-limbed monsters rather than forcing a human body or human clothing onto them.
- Build hair, cheeks, ears, hands and clothing from simple soft masses. Use a few rounded hair clumps rather than detailed individual strands. Preserve distinguishing horns, teeth or claws with readable simplified forms.
- Use densely filled color with understated fine crayon/pastel-like grain. The texture is integrated softly into the colors, not conspicuous scratchy scribbles or white paper gaps. Keep the surface matte and gently powdery, not actual plush, fabric, felt fibers, woven cloth or embroidery.
- Shade rounded volumes softly, with modest warm depth at edges and overlaps. Match the references' simple softly lit illustrated volume; avoid glossy physical rendering, harsh cast shadows, faceted digital painting and plastic toy photography.
- Use a thin, soft, muted dark-brown contour blended into the shading. Avoid a thick dark ink outline or flat vector appearance.
- Keep the supplied design colors while matching the references' warm, restrained saturation and shading. Allow green, blue, gray or any other requested species color; do not force every character into the samples' outfit colors.
- Unless a different eye structure is specified, use small simple dark navy vertical oval eyes, tiny simple nose and mouth where appropriate, and subtle warm cheeks on human faces. Avoid elaborate irises, strong eye sparkles, long lashes, heavy makeup and oversized blush. Follow explicit species-specific eye or facial features.
- Keep ornament and detail density modest. Do not add ornate gold piping, complex seams, realistic folds, hair strands, jewelry or extra weapons without a design reason in the request. Convey personality through the requested expression and silhouette.

VIEW, POSE AND STRUCTURE:
- Follow the requested viewpoint, pose, movement, framing and scene. Do not force a front-facing or symmetrical stance. Maintain the same character proportions and design through front, side, rear or three-quarter views and action poses.
- Make the correct number of limbs for the species, connected naturally. Allow correct perspective and occlusion; do not force hidden hands or legs into view. For humanoids, avoid accidentally missing or extra arms and hands.
- Keep sleeve designs and colors consistent between arms unless asymmetry was requested. Make capes originate at the back shoulders and drape or flow naturally behind the body and arms. Do not tuck a cape into the armpit or merge it into a sleeve.
- Show a natural grip on weapons; keep shafts, handles and head ornaments connected. Follow specified weapon size. For a star staff with no size specified, place the center of a large softly rounded golden five-pointed star beside the face at eye-to-cheek height, rather than beside the top of the hat. Keep the lower shaft extending well below the grip down near the boots. Adapt naturally to the requested pose and viewpoint. Follow an explicitly different staff design or size when requested.
- For a full-body asset, leave clear margin around all limbs, headgear, wings, tails and weapon tips. For a requested close-up or partial crop, follow the intentional framing.
- Default to one isolated full-body character with a transparent background when no scene is specified. Do not paint a black or checkerboard backdrop to simulate transparency. If the user requests a scene or a different background, follow that request while preserving the rendering style.
- For several characters, maintain a coherent scale and rendering style and avoid unwanted overlaps. Follow separate-file or group-layout requests.
- Add no unrequested text, nameplates, frame, ground plane, cast ground shadow, scenery or extra characters.

Before finishing, compare the result visually with the fixed reference images for shape language, proportions, eyes, contour softness, color, texture strength and shading. Correct important visible errors without redesigning unrelated details.

## Edits

For a local edit, replace the opening with an instruction to edit the supplied target image. State the requested change precisely. Keep the target image's identity, composition and unrelated details. Supply that target as an actual image input alongside the fixed style references as needed. Do not interpret the fixed references as additional characters to insert into the edited picture.
